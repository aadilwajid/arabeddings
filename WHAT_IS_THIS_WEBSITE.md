# 🛏️ ARA BEDDINGS - What Is This Website?

## Overview

**ARA BEDDINGS** is a complete, production-ready e-commerce website for selling premium bedding products in Pakistan. It's a fully functional online store with everything you need to sell bed sheets, duvet covers, comforters, blankets, and pillows.

---

## 🎯 What Does It Do?

### For Customers:
1. **Browse Products** - View 9 premium bedding products with beautiful images
2. **Filter & Search** - Find products by category, size, color, price
3. **Select Variants** - Choose size (Single/Double/Queen/King), color, firmness
4. **Add to Cart** - Save items and adjust quantities
5. **Checkout** - Enter Pakistan address, choose shipping, select payment
6. **Pay** - Cash on Delivery or Bank Transfer
7. **Track Orders** - See order status and history
8. **Custom Orders** - Request bulk/special orders for hotels
9. **Wishlist** - Save favorite products
10. **Dark Mode** - Switch between light and dark themes

### For Admins:
1. **Dashboard** - See sales stats, pending orders, revenue
2. **Manage Orders** - Update order status (Pending → Confirmed → Shipped → Delivered)
3. **Verify Payments** - Confirm bank transfers
4. **View Products** - See all products with stock levels
5. **Handle Custom Orders** - Review, quote prices, approve/reject
6. **Configure Shipping** - Set rates for different Pakistan regions
7. **View Customers** - See customer list and order history
8. **Media Library** - Upload and manage product images
9. **Store Settings** - Update store name, logo, bank details
